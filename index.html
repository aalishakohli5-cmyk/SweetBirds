Critically Endangered

Regent Honeyeater

As few as 250 may remain in the wild

The Regent Honeyeater is a black-and-yellow bird found in eastern Australia. It drinks nectar from eucalyptus flowers. Sadly, the loss of its natural habitat has made it critically endangered.

Extinct

Bachman’s Warbler

No confirmed sightings since the 1980s

Bachman’s Warbler was a small bird that lived in the forests of the United States and Cuba. As its forest habitat disappeared, its population declined. It was officially declared extinct in 2023.

Not Currently Endangered

White-rumped Shama

More than 10,000 mature birds are believed to remain

The White-rumped Shama is a forest bird known for its beautiful song. It is not endangered, but its numbers have decreased in some areas because it is often captured and sold as a pet.
